# Weekly Client Review SOP

## Overview
A systematic process to review every client weekly, ensuring nothing falls through the cracks and maintaining visibility across all 15 accounts.

## Owner
**Primary:** Emma (her clients) + Cathy (execution view)
**Oversight:** Joe (reviews in team meeting)

## Trigger
- Every Monday morning
- Feeds into Tuesday weekly team meeting

---

## Why Weekly Reviews Matter

With 15 clients, it's easy to lose track of who needs what. This review ensures:
- Every client gets attention every week
- Blockers surface before they become problems
- The team has shared visibility
- Nothing falls through the cracks

---

## Weekly Review Process

### Step 1: Update Client Tracker (Monday AM)

For each client, update these fields:
- [ ] **Status**: Green / Yellow / Red
- [ ] **Last Touch**: When did we last communicate with them?
- [ ] **Active Work**: What's currently in progress?
- [ ] **Blockers**: Anything stuck?
- [ ] **Next Action**: What's the next thing that needs to happen?

### Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning | Action |
|--------|---------|--------|
| 🟢 Green | On track, no issues | Continue as planned |
| 🟡 Yellow | Minor issue or at risk | Needs attention this week |
| 🔴 Red | Major issue, escalation needed | Discuss in team meeting, may need Joe |

---

## Step 2: Review Each Client (15-20 min)

Go through each client and ask:

### Health Check Questions
1. **Have we communicated with them this week?**
   - If no contact in 7+ days → flag as Yellow
   - If no contact in 14+ days → flag as Red

2. **Are all deliverables on track?**
   - Check ClickUp for overdue tasks
   - Review any pending approvals

3. **Any client feedback or concerns?**
   - Check Slack for any messages needing response
   - Review email inbox

4. **What's the next milestone?**
   - Upcoming campaign launch?
   - Monthly report due?
   - Strategy review scheduled?

### Quick Wins Check
- Is there something small we could do this week to add value?
- Any campaign opportunities (holiday, sale, etc.)?

---

## Step 3: Prepare for Team Meeting

Create a summary of:
- [ ] Total clients: Green / Yellow / Red
- [ ] Any escalations for Joe
- [ ] Key wins from last week
- [ ] Priorities for this week
- [ ] Capacity check (are we overloaded?)

---

## Red Flags to Watch For

- **No communication in 10+ days** → Client may be disengaged
- **Repeated delays on approvals** → Relationship issue?
- **Scope creep without discussion** → Need to reset boundaries
- **Performance declining** → Proactive strategy discussion needed
- **Client contacts changing** → Relationship risk

---

## After Team Meeting

- [ ] Update tracker with any changes from discussion
- [ ] Create ClickUp tasks for agreed actions
- [ ] Send any follow-ups identified
- [ ] Update clients with any relevant info

| Task | Time | Owner |
|------|------|-------|
| Tracker update | 15 min | Emma/Cathy |
| Client-by-client review | 20 min | Emma/Cathy |
| Team meeting prep | 10 min | Emma |
| **Total** | **45 min** | |

---

## Common Issues & Solutions

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Don't have time for review | Do abbreviated version, flag in team meeting |
| Client tracker is out of date | Make updating it part of daily routine |
| Too many Yellow/Red clients | Triage in team meeting, may need Joe help |
| Client seems disengaged | Schedule proactive check-in call |
