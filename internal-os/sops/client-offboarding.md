# Client Offboarding SOP

## Overview
Process for gracefully transitioning a client when the engagement ends, ensuring professionalism and protecting the relationship for potential future work.

## Owner
**Primary:** Emma Hughes (communication, handoff)
**Support:** Cathy Ang (documentation, access)
**Oversight:** Joe (relationship management)

## Trigger
- Client gives notice of termination
- Boring Ecom terminates engagement
- Contract ends and not renewing
- Project-based engagement completes

---

## Offboarding Principles

1. **Leave on good terms** - Today's churned client is tomorrow's referral
2. **Document everything** - Make their transition smooth
3. **Protect our work** - Ensure proper handoff, not abandonment
4. **Learn from it** - Understand why they left

---

## Phase 1: Notification (Day 1)

### When Client Gives Notice

- [ ] Acknowledge receipt professionally
- [ ] Notify Joe immediately (Slack DM)
- [ ] Confirm last day of service
- [ ] Ask for reason (if not provided)

**Response template:**
```
Hi [Name],

Thank you for letting us know. We've really enjoyed working with [Company]
and are proud of what we've accomplished together.

To ensure a smooth transition, I'd like to:
1. Confirm your last day of service is [Date]
2. Schedule a brief handoff call
3. Prepare documentation of your account

Would [Date/Time] work for a 30-minute handoff call?

Best,
[Name]
```

### When Boring Ecom Terminates

- [ ] Joe handles communication
- [ ] Provide adequate notice (per contract)
- [ ] Offer transition support
- [ ] Document reasons internally

### Internal Notification

- [ ] Post in #internal: "Client offboarding: [Name] - Last day [Date]"
- [ ] Update client tracker: Status = "Offboarding"
- [ ] Cancel any scheduled work not needed

---

## Phase 2: Documentation (Days 1-7)

### Create Handoff Document

Prepare comprehensive documentation for the client or their new agency:

**Account Overview**
- [ ] Klaviyo account summary
- [ ] List of all flows (with status)
- [ ] List of all segments
- [ ] Campaign history summary
- [ ] List health metrics

**What We Built**
- [ ] Flows we created/modified
- [ ] Templates we designed
- [ ] Segments we built
- [ ] Any custom integrations

**Current State**
- [ ] What's live and working
- [ ] What's in progress (and status)
- [ ] Known issues or technical debt
- [ ] Recommendations we didn't get to

**Performance Summary**
- [ ] Key metrics during engagement
- [ ] Wins and improvements made
- [ ] Before/after comparisons (if dramatic)

**Access & Credentials**
- [ ] List all tools we have access to
- [ ] Any credentials we manage
- [ ] Integrations we set up

### Export Key Data

- [ ] Export final performance report
- [ ] Download any custom templates
- [ ] Screenshot key dashboards
- [ ] Save anything we might want for case studies (with permission)

---

## Phase 3: Handoff Call (Day 5-10)

### Schedule the Call
- [ ] 30-45 minutes
- [ ] Include: Client contact, their new point person (if applicable)
- [ ] From Boring Ecom: Emma (+ Joe if key account)

### Handoff Call Agenda

1. **Recap of engagement** (5 min)
   - What we accomplished together
   - Key wins

2. **Account walkthrough** (15 min)
   - Current flow status
   - Upcoming campaigns (if any)
   - Important segments to know

3. **Handoff document review** (10 min)
   - Walk through the documentation
   - Answer questions

4. **Transition logistics** (5 min)
   - Access removal timeline
   - Final invoice details
   - How to reach us if questions arise

5. **Thank you & feedback** (5 min)
   - Express gratitude
   - Ask for feedback on our service
   - Offer to stay in touch

---

## Phase 4: Access & Admin (Days 10-14)

### Remove Access

- [ ] Klaviyo: Remove team member access
- [ ] Shopify: Remove staff account
- [ ] Slack: Leave client channel (or archive if Connect)
- [ ] Any other tools

**Timing:** Remove access on last day of service, not before

### ClickUp Cleanup

- [ ] Complete or close all open tasks
- [ ] Archive client project
- [ ] Note final status on any incomplete items

### Google Drive

- [ ] Ensure all docs are in client folder
- [ ] Move folder to "Past Clients" archive
- [ ] Don't delete anything

### Update Tracker

- [ ] Status = "Churned"
- [ ] Churn date = [Date]
- [ ] Churn reason = [Reason]
- [ ] Final notes

---

## Phase 5: Post-Offboarding

### Request Testimonial (If Appropriate)

If the relationship was positive:
```
Hi [Name],

It was great working with you at [Company]. As we wrap up, I wanted to ask -
would you be open to providing a brief testimonial about our work together?

Even a few sentences about your experience would mean a lot and help other
brands understand what we do.

No pressure at all if you'd rather not - I understand either way!

Best,
[Name]
```

### Internal Debrief

Hold a quick team debrief:
- [ ] Why did they leave?
- [ ] What could we have done better?
- [ ] Any patterns with other churned clients?
- [ ] Lessons to apply going forward

### Stay in Touch

- [ ] Add key contact to Joe's LinkedIn (if connected)
- [ ] Add to occasional newsletter (if they consent)
- [ ] Note in CRM for future outreach

## Churn Reason Categories

Track why clients leave:

| Reason | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Budget | Can't afford / cutting costs |
| In-house | Bringing retention in-house |
| New agency | Moving to different agency |
| Business change | Shutting down, pivoting, sold |
| Performance | Not happy with results |
| Relationship | Not happy with service/communication |
| Scope mismatch | Needs didn't match our offering |
| Project complete | Engagement was project-based |

---

## Red Flags During Engagement

Signs a client might churn:
- Reduced communication
- Delayed payments
- Questioning value/ROI
- Key contact leaving
- Reduced scope requests
- Longer approval times

**If you see these:** Notify Joe, schedule proactive check-in

---

## Common Issues & Solutions

| Issue | Solution |
|-------|----------|
| Client wants immediate termination | Joe handles, try to get 2 weeks minimum |
| Client blames us unfairly | Document facts, don't argue, stay professional |
| New agency wants our "secrets" | Provide documentation, not strategy rationale |
| Client owes money | Joe handles collections, still provide handoff |
| Client wants to come back later | Leave door open, note in tracker |
