# Welcome Series Playbook

## Why Welcome Series Matters

The welcome series is your **highest-performing flow**. New subscribers are at peak interest—they just raised their hand. This is your best chance to:

- Convert them to first purchase
- Introduce your brand story
- Set expectations for email content
- Build the relationship early

**Benchmark:** Welcome flows typically drive 3-5% of total Klaviyo revenue with the highest open rates of any flow (45-55%).

---

## Flow Structure

### Recommended: 4-Email Welcome Series

```
Signup
  │
  ▼ (Immediate)
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│ Email 1: Welcome + Offer    │
│ Open: 50-60% │ Click: 8-12% │
└─────────────────────────────┘
  │
  ▼ (Wait 1 day)
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│ Email 2: Brand Story        │
│ Open: 40-50% │ Click: 3-5%  │
└─────────────────────────────┘
  │
  ▼ (Wait 2 days)
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│ Email 3: Social Proof       │
│ Open: 35-45% │ Click: 3-5%  │
└─────────────────────────────┘
  │
  ▼ (Wait 2 days)
┌─────────────────────────────┐
│ Email 4: Offer Reminder     │
│ Open: 30-40% │ Click: 4-7%  │
└─────────────────────────────┘
```

**Total flow duration:** ~6 days

## Email-by-Email Breakdown

### Email 1: Welcome + Offer (Immediate)

**Goal:** Deliver the promised incentive, make first impression

**Subject Line Frameworks:**
- "Welcome to [Brand] + your [X]% off"
- "You're in! Here's your discount"
- "Your [Brand] welcome gift is inside"

**Content Structure:**
1. **Warm welcome** (1-2 sentences)
2. **Deliver the offer** (prominent, clear CTA)
3. **What to expect** (what kind of emails you'll send)
4. **Quick brand intro** (1 sentence about what makes you different)

**Copy Framework:**
```
Welcome to [Brand]!

We're so glad you're here.

As promised, here's your [X% off / $X off / free shipping] code:

[CODE]

[CTA: Shop Now]

You'll hear from us [frequency] with [what: new products, tips, exclusive offers].

[Sign-off]
```

**Design Notes:**
- Make the discount code unmissable
- Single clear CTA
- Mobile-first design
- Keep it short—they haven't earned long emails yet

---

### Email 2: Brand Story (Day 1)

**Goal:** Create emotional connection, share your "why"

**Subject Line Frameworks:**
- "The story behind [Brand]"
- "Why we started [Brand]"
- "Our promise to you"

**Content Structure:**
1. **The origin** (why the brand exists)
2. **The mission** (what you believe in)
3. **What makes you different** (your unique value)
4. **Soft CTA** (shop or learn more)

**Copy Framework:**
```
Hey [First Name],

Most [product category] brands do [X]. We think that's wrong.

That's why we started [Brand].

[2-3 sentences about founding story, mission, or belief]

What makes us different:
- [Differentiator 1]
- [Differentiator 2]
- [Differentiator 3]

[CTA: Explore Our Collection / Learn More]

[Sign-off]
```

**Design Notes:**
- Can include founder photo
- More text-focused than Email 1
- Authentic tone, not salesy

---

### Email 3: Social Proof (Day 3)

**Goal:** Build trust through others' experiences

**Subject Line Frameworks:**
- "Why customers love [Brand]"
- "Don't just take our word for it"
- "See what [X,000] customers are saying"
- "[Star rating] stars across [X] reviews"

**Content Structure:**
1. **Intro** (others love us, you will too)
2. **Reviews/testimonials** (3-5 best ones)
3. **UGC photos** (if available)
4. **Best sellers** (what others are buying)
5. **CTA** (shop with confidence)

**Copy Framework:**
```
Hey [First Name],

Over [X] customers have chosen [Brand]. Here's what they're saying:

[Review 1 - with name/photo if possible]
[Review 2]
[Review 3]

Ready to see what everyone's talking about?

[CTA: Shop Best Sellers]

[Sign-off]
```

**Design Notes:**
- Star ratings are powerful
- Real customer photos > stock
- Include product images with the reviews

---

### Email 4: Offer Reminder (Day 5-6)

**Goal:** Create urgency, convert the holdouts

**Subject Line Frameworks:**
- "Your [X]% off expires soon"
- "Last chance: don't miss your welcome gift"
- "Did you forget something?"
- "Your code is about to expire ⏰"

**Content Structure:**
1. **Urgency** (time is running out)
2. **Restate the offer**
3. **Remove objections** (free returns, guarantee, etc.)
4. **Final CTA**

**Copy Framework:**
```
Hey [First Name],

Just a heads up—your welcome offer expires in [24/48 hours].

Your code: [CODE]

Here's what you'll get:
- [Product benefit 1]
- [Product benefit 2]
- [Free shipping / Free returns / Guarantee]

This is your last chance to save [X]%.

[CTA: Use My Discount]

[Sign-off]
```

**Design Notes:**
- Clear urgency without being aggressive
- Prominent countdown or deadline
- Restate any trust signals

## Conditional Logic

### Already Purchased?

Add a conditional split after Email 1:

```
           Email 1 Sent
                │
        ┌───────┴───────┐
        │               │
   Has Placed      Has NOT Placed
     Order             Order
        │               │
        ▼               ▼
  Exit Flow or      Continue
  Move to Post-     Welcome
   Purchase          Series
```

### Offer Type Variations

| If they signed up via... | Adjust flow to... |
|--------------------------|-------------------|
| Discount popup | Deliver that specific discount |
| Quiz/survey | Reference their quiz results |
| Content/lead magnet | Deliver the content first |
| No incentive | Focus on brand story, skip urgency |

## Timing Considerations

| Email | Timing | Rationale |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| 1 | Immediate | Deliver incentive while interest is hot |
| 2 | +1 day | Give time to read Email 1 |
| 3 | +2 days | Space it out, don't overwhelm |
| 4 | +2 days | Before offer expires, create urgency |

**Total:** ~6 days

**Adjust timing for:**
- High-consideration products → space out more
- Impulse buys → tighten up
- Expensive items → add more emails, more trust-building

---

## Welcome Offer Strategies

| Offer Type | Best For | Considerations |
|------------|----------|----------------|
| % off | Most brands | 10-15% is standard, 20%+ for higher margins |
| $ off | Higher AOV | "$10 off $50+" protects margins |
| Free shipping | If shipping costs are a barrier | May not be compelling enough alone |
| Free gift | Brands with samples/GWP | Great for building goodwill |
| No offer | Premium/luxury brands | Focus on story and exclusivity |

## Optimization Checklist

### A/B Test Ideas

**Subject Lines:**
- With vs. without discount in subject
- Personalization (name) vs. no name
- Emoji vs. no emoji

**Content:**
- Long-form story vs. short-form
- Product-focused vs. brand-focused
- Single CTA vs. multiple options

**Timing:**
- Immediate vs. 30-minute delay for Email 1
- 1 day vs. 2 day delays between emails

### Performance Red Flags

| Issue | Likely Cause | Fix |
|-------|--------------|-----|
| Low open on Email 1 | Bad subject or deliverability | Test subjects, check spam score |
| Low click on Email 1 | Offer not compelling or CTA buried | Make offer more prominent |
| High drop-off after Email 2 | Brand story not resonating | Shorten or make more relatable |
| No conversions | Offer too weak or friction in checkout | Increase offer or review site |

## Welcome Series Checklist

Before launching a welcome series:

- [ ] Offer decided and code created
- [ ] All 4 emails written and designed
- [ ] Mobile preview checked
- [ ] Links tested
- [ ] Conditional split for purchasers added
- [ ] Smart sending enabled
- [ ] UTM tracking added
- [ ] Trigger is correct (list subscription)
- [ ] Filters exclude existing customers (optional)
