# Campaign Calendar Playbook

## Why Campaign Calendars Matter

Flows run on autopilot, but **campaigns** require planning. A campaign calendar ensures:
- Consistent communication with your list
- Strategic timing around holidays and events
- No last-minute scrambles
- Alignment with client's business goals

---

## Campaign Types

### Promotional Campaigns
- Sales and discounts
- Product launches
- Holiday promotions
- Flash sales
- BOGO offers

### Content Campaigns
- Newsletters
- Educational content
- Brand storytelling
- Behind-the-scenes
- User-generated content features

### Lifecycle Campaigns
- Seasonal reminders
- Anniversary/milestone emails
- Re-engagement pushes
- Category-specific sends

---

## Campaign Frequency Guidelines

| List Size | Recommended Frequency |
|-----------|----------------------|
| < 10,000 | 1-2x per week |
| 10,000-50,000 | 2-3x per week |
| 50,000+ | 2-4x per week |

**Balance:** Too few = missed revenue. Too many = unsubscribes.

**Rule of thumb:** At minimum, email your list once per week. Engaged lists can handle more.

## Monthly Planning Process

### Start of Month: Plan Ahead

1. **Review client's business calendar**
   - Any product launches?
   - Sales or promotions planned?
   - Inventory considerations?

2. **Check holiday calendar**
   - What holidays fall this month?
   - Are they relevant to this brand?

3. **Draft campaign calendar**
   - 4-8 campaigns minimum for the month
   - Mix of promotional and content

4. **Get client approval**
   - Share calendar in Slack or call
   - Confirm dates, offers, focus areas

## Segmentation Strategies

### Common Segments for Campaigns

| Segment | Use For |
|---------|---------|
| Full list | Major announcements, big sales |
| Engaged (opened/clicked in 90 days) | Regular campaigns |
| VIPs (top spenders) | Early access, exclusive offers |
| Non-purchasers | Stronger offers, education |
| Recent purchasers | Cross-sell, content (not heavy promo) |
| By product category | Relevant product launches |

### Exclusions to Consider

- Don't email someone who bought today (unless transactional)
- Exclude recent recipients of similar offers
- Exclude unengaged for important campaigns (deliverability)

## BFCM Planning (Special Section)

Black Friday/Cyber Monday is the biggest email period of the year. Plan early.

### BFCM Timeline

**October:**
- Finalize BFCM offers with client
- Plan email calendar (12-20+ emails over the period)
- Design email templates
- Create discount codes

**Early November (1st-3rd week):**
- Tease BFCM ("something big is coming")
- Early access for VIPs/SMS subscribers
- Warm up sending volume gradually

**BFCM Week:**
- Heavy email volume (1-2x per day)
- Black Friday: Launch email, reminder, last chance
- Weekend: Continued sale
- Cyber Monday: Online focus, final push

**Post-BFCM:**
- Thank you to purchasers
- Extended sale (optional)
- Shift to holiday gift guide

### BFCM Campaign Schedule Example

```
Nov 20 (Wed): "Something big is coming" teaser
Nov 21 (Thu): VIP early access (24 hours early)
Nov 22 (Fri - Black Friday): Sale launch
Nov 22 (Fri evening): Reminder
Nov 23 (Sat): Best sellers + sale continues
Nov 24 (Sun): Last day of BF deals
Nov 25 (Mon - Cyber Monday): Cyber Monday launch
Nov 25 (Mon evening): Final hours
Nov 26 (Tue): Extended sale (optional) or thank you
```

## Content Ideas When There's No Sale

Not every email needs a discount. Content campaigns that work:

- **Educational:** How to use products, tips, guides
- **Social proof:** Customer stories, reviews, UGC
- **Behind-the-scenes:** Team intro, process, sourcing
- **Lifestyle:** Styled shoots, use cases, inspiration
- **Community:** Feature customers, user spotlights
- **New arrivals:** No discount, just showcase new products
- **Restocks:** Popular items back in stock
- **Best sellers:** What everyone's buying

## Measuring Campaign Success

### Key Metrics

| Metric | Good | Great |
|--------|------|-------|
| Open Rate | 30-40% | 40%+ |
| Click Rate | 2-3% | 4%+ |
| Unsubscribe Rate | <0.3% | <0.2% |
| Revenue per Email | Varies | Compare to your average |

### Post-Campaign Analysis

After each campaign, note:
- Open rate vs. benchmark
- Click rate vs. benchmark
- Revenue generated
- What subject line was used
- Any learnings for next time
