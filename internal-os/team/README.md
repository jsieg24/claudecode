## Role Definitions

**Primary Focus:** Business development, thought leadership, strategic oversight

| Responsibility | Details |
|----------------|---------|
| **Sales** | All new business development, proposals, closing deals |
| **Marketing** | LinkedIn content, brand building, inbound lead generation |
| **Strategy** | High-level retention strategy for key accounts, innovation |
| **Team Management** | 1:1s, hiring, performance, team development |
| **Client Relationships** | Executive sponsor on escalations, QBRs for key accounts |

**Time Allocation (Target):**
- 30% Sales & new business
- 25% Marketing & LinkedIn
- 25% Client strategy & oversight
- 20% Team management & internal

### Emma Hughes - Sr. Manager of Retention

**Primary Focus:** Own strategy and execution for her portfolio of clients

| Responsibility | Details |
|----------------|---------|
| **Client Strategy** | Retention roadmaps, campaign planning, strategic recommendations |
| **Execution Oversight** | Ensure campaigns launch on time and meet quality standards |
| **Client Communication** | Primary point of contact for her clients |
| **Reporting** | Monthly performance reviews, strategic insights |
| **Collaboration** | Work with Cathy on execution, escalate to Joe as needed |

**Client Ownership:** Emma owns ~7-8 clients end-to-end (strategy + execution)

**Time Allocation (Target):**
- 40% Strategy & planning
- 35% Client communication & meetings
- 15% Execution & QA
- 10% Internal collaboration

## RACI Matrix

**R** = Responsible (does the work)
**A** = Accountable (final decision maker)
**C** = Consulted (provides input)
**I** = Informed (kept in the loop)

### Client Work

| Activity | Joe | Emma | Cathy |
|----------|-----|------|-------|
| New client sales/proposals | R/A | C | I |
| Client onboarding | A | R | R |
| Retention strategy/roadmap | C | R/A | I |
| Flow building (Klaviyo) | I | C | R/A |
| Campaign execution | I | A | R |
| Email/SMS copywriting | C | R | R |
| Monthly reporting | I | A | R |
| Client Slack responses | I | R | R |
| Escalation handling | A | R | C |
| QBR presentations | R | R | C |

### Internal Operations

| Activity | Joe | Emma | Cathy |
|----------|-----|------|-------|

| Process documentation | A | R | R |
| Tool/software decisions | R/A | C | C |
| Weekly team meeting | R | R | R |

### Marketing & Sales
