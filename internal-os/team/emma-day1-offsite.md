## Agenda

### 1. Welcome & Coffee Chat (30 min)

**Cover:**

**Questions for Emma:**

### BREAK (15-30 min)

### 5. Her Role Deep Dive (45 min)
*Set expectations clearly*

**Cover:**
- Sr. Manager of Retention - what this means
- Her client portfolio (which clients she'll own)
- Strategy + execution ownership
- What success looks like in 30/60/90 days

**Questions:**
- What do you think you'll be best at in this role?
- Where do you think you'll need the most support?
- What do you need from me to be successful?
- How do you like to be managed? (Hands-on vs. autonomous)

**30/60/90 Day Expectations:**
| Timeframe | Focus |
|-----------|-------|
| First 30 days | Learn clients, shadow work, understand processes |
| 60 days | Own 3-4 clients independently, contribute to strategy |
| 90 days | Fully own portfolio, driving results, minimal oversight needed |

### 7. How We Work (30 min)

- How do you stay organized?
- Any processes from past roles that worked really well?

### 8. Working Together (20 min)
*Manager/report relationship*

**Questions:**

**Share:**
