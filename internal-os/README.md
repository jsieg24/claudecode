## How This OS Works

### 1. SOPs (Standard Operating Procedures)
Repeatable processes that anyone on the team can follow. These ensure consistency whether Joe, Emma, or Cathy is doing the work.

### 2. Playbooks
Detailed guides for specific retention tactics—welcome flows, abandoned cart, post-purchase, winback, etc.

### 3. Templates
Ready-to-use spreadsheets and docs for client tracking, reporting, and communication.

### 4. Meeting Rhythms
When we meet, what we cover, and how we stay aligned as a team.

## Core Principles

1. **Document everything** - If it's in your head, it should be in this OS
2. **Clients see results, not chaos** - Our internal organization enables client success
3. **Own your lane** - Clear ownership prevents things from falling through cracks
4. **Weekly visibility** - Every client should have clear status every week
5. **Boring is beautiful** - Consistent, repeatable processes beat heroics
