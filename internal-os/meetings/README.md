# Meeting Rhythms & Cadences

> Structured meetings keep the team aligned and clients happy.

---

## Internal Meeting Schedule

### Weekly Team Meeting

**When:** Tuesday, 10:00 AM (30-45 min)
**Who:** Joe, Emma, Cathy
**Where:** Google Meet

**Purpose:**
- Review all client status
- Surface blockers and escalations
- Align on priorities for the week
- Quick wins and celebrations

**Agenda:**

```
## Weekly Team Meeting - [Date]

### 1. Client Review (20 min)
[Pull up client tracker]

- 🟢 Green clients: Quick acknowledgment
- 🟡 Yellow clients: Discuss, assign actions
- 🔴 Red clients: Deep dive, escalation plan

### 2. Blockers & Escalations (5 min)
- What's stuck?
- Who needs help?
- Any client escalations for Joe?

### 3. Priorities This Week (5 min)
- Emma's top 3
- Cathy's top 3
- Joe's focus areas

### 4. Wins & Celebrations (5 min)
- What went well last week?
- Any client feedback to share?

### 5. Any Other Business (5 min)
- Process improvements
- Tool/resource needs
- Upcoming PTO
```

**Prep Required:**
- Emma/Cathy: Update client tracker before meeting
- Joe: Review tracker, note questions

## Client Meeting Cadences

### Standard Client Communication

| Type | Frequency | Who | Purpose |
|------|-----------|-----|---------|
| Slack check-ins | As needed | Emma/Cathy | Quick updates, questions |
| Monthly report | Monthly | Cathy (send), Emma (review) | Performance review |
| Strategy call | Quarterly or as needed | Emma + Client | Roadmap review, strategy discussion |
| QBR | Quarterly | Emma + Joe (key accounts) | Deep dive, relationship building |

---

### Monthly Report Delivery Call (Optional)

**When:** Within 1 week of report delivery (if client wants)
**Duration:** 30 min
**Who:** Emma + Client

**Agenda:**
```
## Monthly Report Walkthrough

### 1. Quick Check-in (5 min)
- How's everything going?
- Any immediate concerns?

### 2. Report Review (15 min)
- Walk through key metrics
- Highlight wins
- Explain any dips
- Answer questions

### 3. What's Next (10 min)
- Next month priorities
- Any upcoming promotions or launches?
- Decisions needed from client?
```

---

### Quarterly Business Review (QBR)

**When:** Quarterly (or bi-annually for smaller accounts)
**Duration:** 45-60 min
**Who:** Emma + Client (Joe joins for key accounts)

**Purpose:**
- Deeper strategic review
- Relationship building
- Identify new opportunities
- Address any concerns

**Agenda:**
```
## Q[X] Business Review - [Client Name]

### 1. Relationship Check-in (10 min)
- How are we doing as a partner?
- Any feedback on our service?
- What's working, what's not?

### 2. Quarter in Review (15 min)
- Key metrics and trends
- Major wins
- Challenges faced
- Work completed

### 3. Strategic Discussion (20 min)
- What's coming for your business?
- New products, promotions, goals?
- How can retention support growth?
- Opportunities we see

### 4. Next Quarter Plan (10 min)
- Proposed priorities
- Any scope changes needed?
- Alignment on direction

### 5. Q&A (5 min)
- Open floor for questions
```

**Prep:**
- Pull quarter-over-quarter metrics
- Prepare slides/deck
- Review client's business news
- Note any concerns to address proactively

## Meeting Best Practices

### Before the Meeting
- [ ] Send agenda 24 hours ahead (client meetings)
- [ ] Prep any materials needed
- [ ] Review previous meeting notes
- [ ] Check for any updates/news about client

### During the Meeting
- [ ] Start on time
- [ ] Follow agenda (but be flexible)
- [ ] Take notes (or assign note-taker)
- [ ] Capture action items clearly
- [ ] End with clear next steps

### After the Meeting
- [ ] Send follow-up email within 24 hours
- [ ] Include action items and owners
- [ ] Update client tracker
- [ ] Create ClickUp tasks for action items

## Calendar Blocking

**Recommended schedule structure:**

| Day | Focus |
|-----|-------|
| Monday | Client tracker update, week planning |
| Tuesday | Team meeting, client work |
| Wednesday | Deep work (minimize meetings) |
| Thursday | Client calls, 1:1s |
| Friday | Wrap-up, prep for next week |

**Protect focus time:** Block 2-3 hours of "deep work" time on calendar to prevent meeting creep.
